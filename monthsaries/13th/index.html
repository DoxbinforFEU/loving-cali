Exhibit 13 — Field Prints

Ten frames,
none of them posed.

Pulled from the month's contact sheet — kept in the order they were taken, not the order that flatters.

01 · 13/24

Close Range

Candlelight, foggy glasses, and no reason to pull the camera back. Some frames don't need staging — just someone close enough to take them.

02 · 13/24

Between Errands

Not a planned stop — just the ten minutes between one appointment and the next, kept anyway.

03 · 13/24

Unposed

Low light, no direction, kept anyway. Some of the best entries were never meant to be entries.

Thirteen months in, and the archive still fills faster than we can index it.

Field note
04 · 13/24

Everyone Who Shows Up

A living room full of people who keep circling back into frame. The record was never just the two of us.

05 · 13/24

Golden Interruption

The sky changed color mid-game and nobody called a timeout. We just angled the shot to catch both.

06 · 13/24

Borrowed Florals

Stopped for flowers that weren't ours to keep, in a mirror that wasn't either. Filed under things we wandered into.

07 · 13/24

No Light To Speak Of

Filed as evidence regardless — proof doesn't need good lighting, just proximity.

08 · 13/24

The Wider Record

Small hands and smaller opinions get filed into the archive too. Family finds its way into every chapter, invited or not.

09 · 13/24

The Ride Home

Barely lit, half-asleep, still logged. Not every entry needs a good camera — some just need to happen.

10 · 13/24

Too Close To Focus

The camera gave up before we did. Correctly so.

End of Chapter 13

Filed, not finished.

Chapter 14 is already collecting frames. This one just closes its cover for now.
